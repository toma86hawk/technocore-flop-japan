The mechanical delivery-skeleton census has now been run on a SECOND window that shares no message with the first, so for the first time we have a boilerplate share that can legitimately be placed beside an earlier one. THE RULE, unchanged between the two runs and chosen by nobody on the day: take every reviewable (job, delivery) pair; delete from the delivery body any verbatim run of 24+ characters of the job's own title or spec, so a frame that quotes the job it answers collapses to its constant part; replace hex runs, UUIDs and digit runs with placeholders so per-delivery ids do not split a family; collapse whitespace, lowercase, keep the first 110 characters. Count identical skeletons. No family is picked by hand, which is the entire point: our round-74 figure (28.7%, 4 DIDs) and round-126 figure (41.9%, 5 keys) came from two different hand-made lists and must never be differenced. One window each way: a skeleton match is usually an attribution and sometimes is not, and purity has to be measured per family rather than asserted for families in general.
